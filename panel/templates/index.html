About this range

Training purpose: hands-on detection and investigation practice against a real OT/ICS stack — a simulated water pump station modeled on real, public 2026 water-sector incidents, not invented threats.

Intended audience: university cybersecurity programs, corporate security training teams, OT asset owners, cyber range instructors, and self-study learners.

Safety limitations: simulated environment only. Attack tooling is hard-bound to this range's own address space — see SECURITY.md. Never point anything here at real equipment.

Supported scenarios: {{ scenarios|length }} — recon, unauthorized command, manipulation of view, and PLC logic modification. See coverage-matrix.md for exactly what's detected and what isn't.

Local-only architecture: this control panel binds to 127.0.0.1 only and never leaves your machine. Learner profiles, progress, and scoring are stored in this installation's local SQLite database.

Licensing: Apache-2.0 for all original code; third-party components (OpenPLC, Zeek, Suricata, Grafana) retain their own licenses — see docs/licenses.md.

Model fidelity: the simulated process is educational, not an engineering-accurate hydraulic model — tank/pump/demand numbers are tuned to be legible on a dashboard, not for real process engineering analysis.

Local training progress only: completion reports generated here use this installation's SQLite records and are not tamper-resistant or suitable as formal certification evidence.
